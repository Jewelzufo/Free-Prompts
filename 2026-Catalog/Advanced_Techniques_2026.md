## 🎨 Multimodal Contextual Anchoring

**Enterprise Use:** Create cohesive marketing content across multiple platforms.

```plaintext
Given [image description] and theme "[context]", generate:
1. 3 social media captions (emojis optional)
2. 2 blog post angles linking visual elements to [industry trend]
3. 1 video script storyboard maintaining brand voice
```

**Example:**

```plaintext
Given an image of a bustling urban market and the theme "sustainable living", generate:
1. 3 social media captions (emojis optional)
   - "🌿 Shop local, live green! Discover sustainable goodies at your neighborhood market. #SustainableLiving #LocalMarkets"
   - "From farm to table: Embrace fresh, local produce for a healthier planet. 🍎🍅 #EcoFriendly #SupportLocal"
   - "Sustainability starts in your community. Explore our market for eco-friendly finds! ♻️ #GoGreen"
2. 2 blog post angles linking visual elements to sustainable living trends
   - "Urban Markets: The Heartbeat of Sustainable Communities"
   - "How Local Markets Are Leading the Zero-Waste Revolution"
3. 1 video script storyboard maintaining brand voice
   - Scene 1: [Visual of market stalls with fresh produce]
     Narrator: "In the heart of our city, a revolution is brewing."
   - Scene 2: [Close-up of reusable bags and containers]
     Narrator: "Discover how small choices make a big impact."
   - Scene 3: [Smiling vendor handing a product to a customer]
     Narrator: "Join us in the journey towards sustainability, one purchase at a time."
```

**Enterprise Use Case:**
**Industry:** Retail & E-commerce
**Application:** A sustainability-focused retail brand uses this template to create cohesive content across multiple platforms, ensuring consistent messaging about sustainable living while leveraging visuals from local markets.

## 👤 Expert Persona

**Enterprise Use:** Develop strategic plans for operational improvements.

```plaintext
As [Expert Role] facing [Challenge], outline:
1. 3 priority considerations
2. 2 unconventional solutions
3. 1 implementation roadmap
```

**Example:**

```plaintext
As a healthcare administrator facing patient wait time reduction, outline:
1. 3 priority considerations
   - Patient satisfaction and experience
   - Staff efficiency and morale

   - Implement AI-powered triage chatbots for initial patient assessments.

3. 1 implementation roadmap
   - Phase 1: Pilot chatbot system in one department (2 months)
   - Phase 2: Integrate predictive analytics and train staff (3 months)
   - Phase 3: Full-scale rollout and continuous improvement (ongoing)
```

**Enterprise Use Case:**
**Industry:** Healthcare
**Application:** A hospital management team uses this template to develop a strategic plan for reducing patient wait times, considering both technological innovations and operational efficiencies.

## 🔄 Cognitive Chain Refinement

**Enterprise Use:** Refine policy recommendations with data-driven insights.

```plaintext
First Pass: [Initial Answer]
Analysis: Identify 2 factual gaps and 1 logical weakness
Second Pass: [Improved Answer]
Final Verification: Cross-reference with [Source 1] and [Source 2]
```

**Example:**

```plaintext
First Pass: "The best way to reduce carbon emissions is to switch to electric vehicles."
Analysis: Identify 2 factual gaps and 1 logical weakness
   - Factual Gap 1: Lack of data on current emissions from transportation sector.
   - Factual Gap 2: No consideration of electricity source for electric vehicles.
   - Logical Weakness: Overlooks other significant sources of emissions (e.g., industrial, agricultural).
Second Pass: "To effectively reduce carbon emissions, a multi-faceted approach is needed, including transitioning to electric vehicles (EVs), improving public transportation, and investing in renewable energy sources for electricity generation. EVs are most beneficial when charged with renewable energy."
Final Verification: Cross-reference with IPCC Report 2023 and U.S. Energy Information Administration data.
```

**Enterprise Use Case:**
**Industry:** Energy & Environmental Policy
**Application:** An environmental policy think tank uses this template to refine their recommendations for reducing carbon emissions, ensuring their proposals are data-driven and comprehensive.

## ⏳ Temporal Contextualization

**Enterprise Use:** Keep content current with industry developments.

```plaintext
Analyze [Current Event] and update [Existing Content] by:
1. Adding 3 relevant data points
2. Modifying 2 outdated assertions
3. Creating 1 engagement hook tied to trending discussions
```

**Example:**

```plaintext
Analyze the recent advancements in AI technology (2026) and update the blog post "The Future of AI" by:
1. Adding 3 relevant data points
   - New AI models with enhanced reasoning capabilities released in early 2026.
   - Increased adoption of AI in healthcare diagnostics by 40% in the past year.
   - Recent regulations on AI ethics and governance enacted in the EU and US.
2. Modifying 2 outdated assertions
   - "AI is mostly theoretical" -> "AI applications are now widespread across industries."
   - "AI development is unregulated" -> "Governments are actively shaping AI governance frameworks."
3. Creating 1 engagement hook tied to trending discussions
   - "Join the conversation on how the latest AI innovations are transforming our daily lives and what this means for the future of work. #AIRevolution #FutureOfWork"
```

**Enterprise Use Case:**
**Industry:** Technology & Media
**Application:** A technology news platform uses this template to keep their existing content up-to-date with the latest developments in AI, ensuring their readers have access to current and relevant information.


```plaintext

```

## 📊 Few-Shot Prompting

**Enterprise Use:** Train AI chatbots for consistent customer support.

```plaintext
Given the following examples:
Example 1: [Input] -> [Output]
Example 2: [Input] -> [Output]
Example 3: [Input] -> [Output]

Now, for this new input: [New Input], generate an appropriate output.
```

**Example:**

```plaintext
Given the following examples of customer reviews and responses:
Example 1:
   Input: "The product was okay, but delivery took too long."
   Output: "Thank you for your feedback. We're sorry for the delay and appreciate your patience."
Example 2:
   Input: "I love this product! It works perfectly."
   Output: "We're glad to hear you're enjoying the product! Thank you for your support."
Example 3:
   Input: "The item was defective. Very disappointed."
   Output: "We're sorry to hear about the issue. Please contact our support team for a replacement or refund."

Now, for this new input: "The app is user-friendly but lacks some features.", generate an appropriate output.
   Output: "Thank you for your feedback! We're happy you find the app user-friendly. Your input on features is valuable to us as we continue to improve."
```

**Enterprise Use Case:**
**Industry:** Customer Support & Service
**Application:** A customer support team uses this template to train their AI chatbot to respond appropriately to various types of customer feedback, ensuring consistent and helpful responses.


**Example:**

```plaintext

2. **Energy Independence**: Decreases reliance on fossil fuels and volatile energy markets.
3. **Economic Benefits**: Creates jobs in the renewable energy sector and reduces long-term energy costs.
4. **Sustainability**: Ensures energy resources are available for future generations.
Therefore, investing in renewable energy sources is crucial for environmental, economic, and social reasons.
```

**Enterprise Use Case:**

## 🏗️ Scaffolding

**Enterprise Use:** Guide structured content creation processes.

```plaintext
Follow this structure to complete the task:
1. [Step 1]
2. [Step 2]
3. [Step 3]

Now, complete the task: [Task Description]
```

**Example:**

```plaintext
Follow this structure to write a book review:
1. Brief summary of the book's main theme and plot.
2. Analysis of the author's writing style and character development.
3. Personal reflection on the book's impact and who would enjoy reading it.

Now, complete the task: Write a book review for "The Silent Patient" by Alex Michaelides.
1. Brief summary: "The Silent Patient" is a psychological thriller about a woman who shoots her husband and then never speaks again. The story follows a criminal psychotherapist determined to uncover the truth behind her silence.
2. Analysis: Alex Michaelides employs a gripping narrative style with short chapters that build suspense. The character development is intricate, with unexpected twists that keep readers engaged.
3. Personal reflection: This book is a page-turner that challenges perceptions of truth and reality. Ideal for fans of psychological thrillers and mystery novels.
```

**Enterprise Use Case:**
**Industry:** Publishing & Media
**Application:** A book club uses this template to guide members in writing structured and insightful book reviews, enhancing their critical thinking and writing skills.

## 🔄 Self-Consistency

**Enterprise Use:** Ensure consistent responses aligned with company values.

```plaintext
Given the previous responses:
1. [Response 1]
2. [Response 2]

Maintain consistency with these responses while addressing:
[New Input]
```

**Example:**

```plaintext
Given the previous responses:
1. "Our company prioritizes customer satisfaction and quality products."
2. "We are committed to sustainable practices in all our operations."

Maintain consistency with these responses while addressing: "How does your company handle product returns and recycling?"
   Output: "Our company handles product returns by offering hassle-free return policies and ensuring that returned items are either refurbished or recycled responsibly. We maintain our commitment to sustainability by using eco-friendly packaging and promoting recycling programs for our products."
```

**Enterprise Use Case:**
**Industry:** Customer Service & Sustainability
**Application:** A customer service team uses this template to ensure their responses to customer inquiries are consistent with the company's values and policies, building trust and reliability.
