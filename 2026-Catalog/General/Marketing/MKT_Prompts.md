# Marketing Prompt Templates

## 1. Product Launch Announcement
**Prompt:**
"Write a compelling product launch announcement for **[Product Name]**, a **[Product Category]** designed for **[Target Audience]**. Highlight its key features: **[Feature 1]**, **[Feature 2]**, and **[Feature 3]**. Emphasize how it solves **[Pain Point]** and why it stands out from competitors like **[Competitor 1]** and **[Competitor 2]**. Use a **[Tone: e.g., enthusiastic, professional, casual]** tone and include a call-to-action to **[Desired Action: e.g., pre-order, sign up, visit website]**."

**Example Use Case:**
- **Product Name:** EcoClean Toothbrush
- **Product Category:** Sustainable oral care
- **Target Audience:** Eco-conscious consumers
- **Features:** Biodegradable bristles, bamboo handle, ergonomic design
- **Pain Point:** Plastic waste from traditional toothbrushes
- **Competitors:** Colgate, Oral-B
- **Tone:** Enthusiastic
- **Desired Action:** Pre-order now
