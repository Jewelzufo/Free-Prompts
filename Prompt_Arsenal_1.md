## Usage Guide

### Getting Started
1. Browse the categories to find the template that best suits your needs
2. Copy the prompt and replace the placeholder values (in [BRACKETS]) with your specific information
3. Paste the completed prompt into your preferred AI tool
4. Review and refine the AI's response as needed

### Tips for Effective Use
- Be specific when filling in placeholders
- Combine templates when addressing complex problems
- Save frequently used templates with your own customizations
- Consider the context and limitations of your AI tool when using these templates
