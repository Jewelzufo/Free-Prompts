### **Template 1: Legal Contract Drafting**  
```text
Generate a **legally precise** {**formal**} response to the following prompt:  
**[INPUT]**: {**[CLIENT REQUIREMENTS]**}  
**Task**: {**[CONTRACT TYPE]**}  
**Desired Output**: {**[LEGAL DOCUMENT]**}  
**Requirements**:  
* {**[PARTIES]**}: identify all involved entities with full legal names  
* {**[TERMS]**}: define payment, duration, and termination clauses  
* {**[CONFIDENTIALITY]**}: include NDAs or data protection provisions  
* {**[GOVERNING LAW]**}: specify jurisdiction and dispute resolution  
**Style and Tone**: {**professional**} {**unambiguous**}  
**Additional Guidance**:  
* {**[BOILERPLATE]**}: incorporate standard legal clauses  
* {**[COMPLIANCE]**}: ensure adherence to [REGULATORY FRAMEWORK]  
* {**[RISK MITIGATION]**}: address liability limitations  
Provide a response that is **600-800 words** long.  
```
